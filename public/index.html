Using Corkboard

Projects

  • A project holds its own boards and PDFs. Open one from the Projects view.
  • ＋ Add adds sources to the open project: PDFs, web pages (saved as PDF snapshots), images, HTML, text and Markdown files, or PDFs from the library. A PDF in two projects shares its snippets and sections.
  • Open in the PDF library edits a PDF with no project: sections, snippets, notes and details. They come with the PDF into every project.
  • Deleting a project moves it and its boards to data/trash. Its PDFs stay in the PDF library, unless you tick the box to trash the PDFs that only this project uses.
  • PDF library lists every PDF, with the projects that use it. Add a PDF to a project there, or move it to the trash.
  • Board library lists every board. Open a board, move it to another project, or move it to the trash.

Read

  • Text snippet: select text in the PDF and pick a colour. Type a note in the box that opens. Enter saves it.
  • Area snippet: switch to Area (or hold ⌥) and drag a box around a figure, table or image.
  • Edit a snippet: click it in the PDF, or edit its note in the sidebar.
  • Layers and links: make a snippet inside another (a term inside a paragraph). Click it and choose ↗ Link to… to link it to its definition. Hover over a linked term to read the definition. On a board, linked and layered cards get arrows when you place them.
  • Sections: select a heading and pick Section, Sub-section or Level 3. Or use “＋ Section at this page” in the Sections tab. Snippets show the section they are in.

Board

  • Sort: drag snippets from the tray onto the board. Double-click a tray item to drop it in the centre.
  • Groups: Options → Group by document puts each PDF's cards in a frame of their own. Options → New group adds an empty frame. Drag a frame's title bar to move it with its cards, drag its corner to resize, double-click the title to rename.
  • Look first: click a tray item. Its card comes to the centre, or a preview opens there with ＋ Place on board. ← → step through the tray. Esc closes it.
  • Back to the source: click the “file · p.N” line on a card. The PDF opens at that spot.
  • Cards: double-click empty space for a new card. Double-click any card to add a board note to it.
  • Connect: hover a card and drag one of its edge dots onto another card.
  • Sub-links: snippets inside a card's snippet show as highlighted words. Link a word to a word in another snippet (Link to… in the reader), and the arrow joins the two highlighted words. Hover a word to light its arrow. Click a linked word to see its target, and the links into it, in a popup with Open in PDF and Place on board.
  • Review projects: Projects → ＋ New review. Add the PDF to check, highlight its passages, and link each one to snippets in other PDFs. The Links tab lists every passage with what it links to.
  • Highlight: in the reader, pick Highlight and a colour, then select text. It is coloured at once and is not a snippet. Click it to recolour, make it a snippet or delete it.
  • Right-click: on the board, cards, connections and zones have a menu: edit, colour, reverse, delete and more.
  • Notes: right-click the empty board and pick Add custom card (or press N). A new card stays even with no text. Right-click a card for its menu, or double-click a note to write in it. A toolbar gives headings, bold, italic, lists, tasks, quotes and three text sizes. Click a task's box to tick it.
  • Zones: pick Zone (Z) and drag a box on the board. Cards inside it move with it. Click the dot in its title to change the colour.
  • Footnotes: a snippet with a footnote mark carries the footnote from the foot of its page. Refresh text adds them to older snippets.
  • Snippets across pages: repeated page headers and footers are left out by themselves. Select one and click "Skip as header/footer" to mark it by hand.
  • Reference tags: select a citation or document name and click 🔖 Reference. It gets its own colour, and you pick what it links to: another PDF, a snippet or a whole document.
  • Highlight words on a card: pick Highlight, then select words in a card's text and pick a colour. The highlight stays on the card, not in the PDF. Click a highlighted word (with Highlight on) to link it, change its colour or remove it. "Pick on the board instead" links it to a word or card you click.
  • Resize a card: drag its corner grip, its right edge or its bottom edge. Long text scrolls inside a short card. Double-click the corner to fit the height to the text again. A picture card keeps the shape of its picture.
  • Annotate a connection: click it. A bar opens with a label, a note, the direction (→ ← ↔ —), the colour, the line style (dashed, dotted, double, track, glow…), the width, and delete. Enter on a selected connection goes to its label.
  • Travel: double-click an arrow. The view moves to the card at the other end.
  • Focus: select a card and press 1, 2 or 3. Only cards within that many links stay visible. 0 or Esc shows all.
  • Structured layout: press L. Each group gets a lane (a card's group, else its colour). The busiest lane follows document order. Other lanes line up with the cards they link to. Drag a lane's header card to move the lane.
  • Export: Options → Export gives one HTML file to share. It can include the source PDFs. A copy goes to data/exports.
  • Circuit arrows: Options → Arrows → Circuit gives straight lines with 90° turns.
  • Gravity: press G. Linked cards pull together, cards push apart and same colours gather. Drag a card and the rest follow. ⌘Z returns the layout from before.
  • Draw: pick Pen, then draw on a card, a picture or the board. Erase removes strokes.
  • Move around: drag empty space or scroll to pan. Pinch or ⌘+scroll to zoom.
  • Keys: ⌘F find in the PDF · V select · H highlight words · P pen · E erase · Z zone · N card · F fit · G gravity · L layout · 1–3 focus · Del delete · ⌘Z undo

Rename, delete, arrow style, layout and the snippet tray are under Options. Press ? for this help.

Two screens: Options → Open the other view in a new window. Read in one window and sort in the other. They stay in sync.
